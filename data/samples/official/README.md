# Official Sample Dataset

This dataset is the larger MVP test dataset for backend, GA and frontend demo work.

It is not the normal source for a real timetable run. In normal operation, the
Training Department uploads and confirms its own complete seven-file CSV batch.

It contains generated/anonymized data only:

- 20 lecturers with realistic Vietnamese names.
- 31 rooms.
- 56 configured time slots from Monday to Sunday.
- 120 course sections.
- 105 academic-calendar dates across 15 weeks.

With one regular weekly meeting per course section, this dataset targets about
1,500-3,000 dated occurrences after calendar expansion. The current seeded GA
demo produces 120 base assignments and about 1,724 normal dated occurrences,
plus skipped holiday/non-teaching sessions.

Do not replace this with real private university or student data.
